Celeste

Celeste is a private desktop tool used by a single person to publish their own content to their own social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest).

Privacy Policy

Celeste runs only on the owner's computer. It does not have users other than its owner, does not collect, sell or share personal data, and has no servers. Access tokens granted by the platforms are stored encrypted on the owner's computer (Windows Credential Manager) and are used only to upload content the owner chooses. Content and data can be deleted at any time by removing the app and revoking access from each platform's settings.

Terms of Service

Celeste is provided for personal use by its owner. It publishes only content created and approved by the owner and complies with each platform's terms.
